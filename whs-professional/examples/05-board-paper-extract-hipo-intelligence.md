# Board Paper — Safety Performance Q3 FY26 (Extract)

**For**: Board of Directors
**Period**: 1 January – 31 March 2026
**Prepared by**: Group Head of WHS
**Date**: 27 April 2026

---

## Safety performance snapshot

| Metric | Q3 FY26 | Rolling 12m | Prior year RY | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRIFR | 4.2 | 5.1 | 6.8 | <5.0 |
| LTIFR | 1.3 | 1.6 | 2.1 | <2.0 |
| HiPo rate | 2.4 | 2.1 | 2.9 | n/a — investigative metric |
| Fatalities | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 |

Rolling 12-month TRIFR continues a downward trend (−25% on prior year); the
quarter's TRIFR is the lowest single-quarter result in three years. LTIFR is
within target.

The single fatality in the prior reporting year remains an open coronial
matter; ongoing engagement with the Coroner's Court is detailed under
Regulatory and Compliance.

## HiPo intelligence

23 HiPo events were recorded in Q3 FY26 (rate 2.4 per million hours on
approximately 9.5 million hours worked in the quarter; rolling 12-month 2.1).
Distribution by critical risk:

| Critical risk | HiPo count | % of total | vs prior period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile plant / pedestrian interface | 7 | 30% | +3 |
| Working at height | 5 | 22% | +1 |
| Electrical safety | 4 | 17% | −1 |
| Confined space | 2 | 9% | −1 |
| Other (combined) | 5 | 22% | −2 |

The mobile plant / pedestrian cluster is the single most important pattern in
the quarter. Six of the seven incidents originated in distribution centres;
four involved forklift reversing without 360° check. The cluster is a
precursor pattern — incidents that did not cause harm this quarter are the
data that informs whether the system is trending toward a foreseeable serious
event (Hopkins, *Failure to Learn*).

Investigation completion on time (HiPo investigations within 30 days): 87%
(target 90%). Three investigations are overdue beyond 45 days; named contracts
have been escalated to BU GMs.

## Critical risk status

CCV completion across the eight critical risks for the quarter is 91% against
plan. Two critical risks are below 80% completion (Working at Height in the
NSW Property portfolio, and Confined Space in the QLD Services portfolio) —
named action with the relevant Critical Risk Owners.

A heatmap of critical risk × business unit is provided at Appendix B.

## Program performance

SafeStart Summer 2026 program completed in March 2026. 89% workforce
participation across the 8-week program; 14% increase in hazard report rate
during and following the program in participating BUs. Independent review
(Appendix C) recommends continuation with topic refresh.

## Regulatory and compliance

- Zero prohibition notices issued during the quarter
- One improvement notice (SafeWork NSW) — resolved within timeframe; learning
  bulletin issued to affected BUs
- One enforceable undertaking discussion concluded with WHSQ; final agreement
  expected Q4 FY26 (separate paper to Board)
- The (prior-year) industrial manslaughter inquiry remains open; legal
  privilege management continues; in-camera Board update available on request

## Outlook and decisions required

The Board is asked to consider three matters:

1. **Mobile plant / pedestrian capital uplift** — approval of $2.1M FY26
   capital program to retrofit reverse cameras and proximity-warning systems
   across the distribution fleet. Business case at Appendix D.

2. **Group-wide critical risk verification platform** — endorsement of the
   commitment to a single platform replacing four legacy systems; full
   business case to Q1 FY27 Board.

3. **Coronial recommendation engagement** — endorsement of the proposed
   public response position on the prior-year coronial inquest; legal advice
   has been received and is summarised in Appendix E (privileged).

A separate paper on each matter is included in the Board pack.
