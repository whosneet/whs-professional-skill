## Themes and so-what

**Theme 1 — Traffic Management Plan drift across the DC network.** Four of
the eight April HiPos involve the Mobile Plant & Pedestrian Interaction
critical risk; three are forklift events in distribution centres. ICAM analysis of the
Sydney DC event (INC-2026-04-0237) identified that the Traffic Management
Plan had been routinely bypassed for "spot-check" stocktake activity for 18
months. Subsequent walks at Brisbane DC found a similar pattern. This is a network problem, not a Sydney problem.

*Required action*: Group WHS to commission a TMP application audit across
all DC sites by 30 June 2026. *Owner*: Group GM WHS. *Reports to*:
ELT June meeting.

**Theme 2 — Working at height anchor verification.** Both April height
HiPos involved workers connecting to anchors that had not been confirmed via
the site anchor register. Anchor register accessibility is variable across
the portfolio — some sites have current registers, others have not been
audited in 12+ months.

*Required action*: Property Services to complete anchor register audit across
all sites in scope by 31 July 2026. *Owner*: Head of Property Services.

**Theme 3 — Chemical handling reporting culture (positive signal).** The
April hazardous chemicals HiPo was reported by the worker involved within
30 minutes of the event, with no injury occurring. This is the third instance
in six months where worker-initiated early reporting has prevented adverse
outcomes. The hazard report rate at the involved BU has increased 22% since
the SafeStart Summer program. *Recognise* in the next Group safety
communication.
